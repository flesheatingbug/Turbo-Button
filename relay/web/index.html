PCs connected now

A PC only appears while Turbo Button is running on it. "Standby" PCs stay listed for your phone until Turbo Button is closed on them.

Device reports

Reports your phone has saved here, newest first. Removing a device deletes its reports and drops it from the relay.

Phones

In the Turbo Button app: ⚙ → Relay server → Pair with relay, and scan this. It works once and expires in minutes.

Agent setup

Easiest: download Turbo Button with this relay already built in. Nothing else to copy; it keeps the settings when it updates itself.

Windows (TurboButton.exe) Windows, office PC (standby) Linux (AppImage + conf, zip) Rescue USB maker (dead/no-boot PC)

The first download takes a moment while the relay fetches the latest build from GitHub. If you make a new relay key below, download again: older copies stop connecting.

Or save this as turbobutton.conf next to TurboButton.exe / the AppImage on your USB stick. The agent then also connects through this relay, so your phone can reach it from anywhere.


      
For office PCs you want to reach while Turbo Button runs on them, add a line standby=yes: they stay paired with your phone and show up in the app's PC list.
