How it works

Plug in the stick, scan a code, work from the couch.

Run the agent

Double-click TurboButton.exe (or the AppImage on Linux) from your USB stick. A QR code appears on the PC.

Scan the code

Open the phone app and scan it. The PC and phone pair over your Wi-Fi — or your relay, from anywhere.

Scan & plan

Run a scan, let AI build a plan, and review what it suggests. Nothing runs without your tap.

Fix & report

Approve the fixes, watch progress live, and hand the customer a clean before/after report.

Rescue a dead PC

Won't boot? Blue-screening? Boot it from a USB and read it off the disk.

  • Boot from a rescue USB and read the offline Windows, read-only.
  • Blue-screen codes pulled from the crash dumps, in plain language.
  • Won't-boot causes — corrupt registry, missing boot files, stuck updates — and what's recoverable.
  • Reset a forgotten password on a local Windows or Linux account.
  • Drive health and SMART, so you back up a failing disk first.

Read-only by default — nothing on the patient disk changes until you choose a fix. Can't defeat BitLocker or a Microsoft-account login (those are encrypted).
